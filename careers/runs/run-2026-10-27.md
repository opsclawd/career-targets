## Method

1. Read `careers/careers_registry.csv` (403 data rows) and `careers/jobs_registry.csv` (944 data rows) before updating; pulled with `git pull --ff-only origin main` first.
2. Crawled the full Built In Calgary job board including the complete remote board (`builtincalgary.org/jobs` pages plus the remote-only board), then pulled every posting's JSON-LD `JobPosting` block for title, company, posted date, employment type, `jobLocation`, and employer link.
3. Resolved each discovered company to a canonical employer/ATS page: Ashby, Greenhouse, Lever, SmartRecruiters and Workable board APIs were probed by company slug; failures fell back to employer `/careers` pages discovered from the Built In company page's outbound links.
4. Probed ATS boards directly for every company name in the registry (163 boards hit, `/tmp/probe_hits.json`) and re-scraped all matching boards for new postings.
5. Title filter: senior/staff/principal/lead/intermediate + software/backend/full stack/platform/SRE/AI-ML engineering. Rejected management, director, analyst, product, design, mobile-only, test/QA-only, data-analyst, sales/support, legal, consulting and intern/junior/student roles.
6. Location bucketing: Calgary/Airdrie/Cochrane/Rocky View -> `Calgary AB`; Alberta/Edmonton -> `Alberta AB`; standalone `CAN`/`Canada` and explicit Canada-remote -> `Remote Canada`. US-only, Europe-only and non-Canada remote roles excluded.
7. Deduped by exact job URL first, then by normalized company+title+location, then by a level-insensitive title core (senior/staff/principal/lead stripped) so that the same role found on a second board (e.g. G2i `AI Training - Canada` vs `AI Training - CA`, Elastic Principal vs Senior Search Algorithms) was counted once. ATS job URLs preferred over Built In Calgary URLs.
8. Verified every new job URL and every new careers URL returns HTTP 200 before writing.
9. Historical rows preserved: no deletions in either registry.
