## Method

1. Read `careers/careers_registry.csv` (453 data rows) and `careers/jobs_registry.csv` (1132 data rows) before updating; `git pull --ff-only origin main` was issued explicitly because the `origin/main` refspec is missing on the remote (the `origin/main` form fails with `fatal: 'origin/main' does not appear to be a git repository`).
2. Re-probed every registry company against the ATS boards (Ashby, Greenhouse, Greenhouse EU, Lever, Workable, SmartRecruiters, Dover) — 1,310 slug/board combinations, 161 boards hit, 8,780 posting records — and diffed against the job registry.
3. Discovery sweep on Built In Calgary: full board crawl for `software engineer` (953 unique postings from 417 companies), each posting parsed from its JSON-LD `JobPosting` block (title, hiring organization, `jobLocation`, `datePosted`). Location mix: `Canada` 673, `CA` 202, `Calgary, AB, CAN` 47, `CAN` 13, `Alberta, AB, CAN` 3.
4. Fuzzy-matched the 199 unmatched Built In company names against the registry (normalized name, token-subset, and 0.88 sequence-ratio passes) to avoid duplicate entries under alternate names; 55 new employers survived that filter plus company-level vetting.
5. Resolved a canonical careers page for every new company: 32 direct ATS boards (slug-probed and title-verified against the employer name) and 23 direct employer careers pages (domain + path probe, then page-title cross-check against the company name). Aggregator URLs (LinkedIn, Indeed, Eluta, Job Bank) were never used as canonical pages.
6. Title filter: senior/staff/principal/lead/distinguished/intermediate + software/backend/full stack/platform/SRE/DevOps/data/AI-ML/security engineering. Rejected management-only, analyst, QA/test-only, developer-advocate, sales/support, legal, intern/junior/student, mobile-only, advisory and LATAM/US-East-Coast roles.
7. Location bucketing: Calgary/Airdrie/Cochrane/Okotoks -> `Calgary AB`; Alberta/Edmonton -> `Alberta AB`; `CAN`/`Canada` and Canadian cities -> `Canada`; explicit Canada-remote -> `Remote Canada`. US-only, Europe-only and non-Canada remote roles excluded.
8. Deduped by exact job URL first, then normalized company + level-insensitive title core (senior/staff/principal/lead/II stripped) so the same role re-listed on a second board counted once; 74 of the 105 rows are Built In-sourced and 31 come from direct ATS boards.
9. Verified every new job URL (105/105 live) and every new careers URL (54/55 live) before writing.
10. Historical rows preserved: no deletions in either registry. `Xsolla` was re-flagged as `rejected` (crypto/Web3) and its postings skipped.
